Privacy Policy

StepQuest: The Walking Crusade — Last updated July 24, 2026

This policy explains what information StepQuest: The Walking Crusade ("StepQuest", "the app", "we") collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. StepQuest is a fitness role-playing game: you earn in-game currency and abilities by walking and logging meals, then use those rewards in a dice-based combat game.

Information We Collect

CategoryWhat we collectWhy
Account information Email address and password (or, if you sign in with Google/GitHub, basic profile info from that provider) To create and secure your account, and sync your progress across devices
Fitness & activity data Step count, walking distance, and calories burned, read from your device's pedometer or Android Health Connect Core gameplay — this is how you earn in-game gold, dice rolls, and experience
Food photos & descriptions Photos you take or select, or text descriptions you type, when logging a meal Sent to our AI calorie-estimation service to estimate calories for your food log. Photos are processed for that single request and are not stored on our servers afterward — only the estimated calorie count and food name are saved to your account.
Purchase & subscription data Records of in-app purchases and subscription status (e.g. whether you've purchased the Pro subscription or an in-game currency bundle) To grant you the items/features you've purchased, and to process billing through Google Play
Advertising identifiers Your device's advertising ID, used by Google AdMob to serve banner ads To display ads that help fund free use of the app (not shown if you have an active Pro subscription)
Push notification token A device token used to deliver reminder notifications To send you optional gameplay reminders (e.g. daily step reminders)

Third-Party Services We Use

We don't run our own servers for most of this — instead we use the following third-party services, each of which processes a specific slice of the data above on our behalf:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, user authentication, and backend functions. Your account info and game progress are stored here.
  • Anthropic — provides the AI model used to estimate calories from food photos/descriptions. Photos are sent to Anthropic's API for a single analysis request and are not retained by us afterward.
  • Google AdMob — serves banner ads within the app.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription and purchase status.
  • Google Play Billing — processes all real-money payments; we never see or store your payment card details.
  • Expo — provides the push notification delivery infrastructure.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not use your food photos, step data, or account info for any purpose beyond providing the app's features described above.
  • We do not share your data with third parties except the service providers listed above, each of whom processes it only to perform the specific function we use them for.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your account data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and all associated game data at any time:

  • In the app: open the Shop tab → Settings → Account → "Delete My Account". This immediately and permanently deletes your account, game progress, and daily activity history.
  • Without the app installed: email appsmovingmeforward@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, and we will delete it on your behalf within a reasonable time.
Deleting your StepQuest account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription or refund past purchases. Manage or cancel subscriptions separately through Google Play (Play Store app → Profile → Payments & subscriptions).

Children's Privacy

StepQuest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the email below and we will delete it.

Security

Data is transmitted using HTTPS/TLS encryption. Your game data is protected with database-level access rules (Row Level Security) that restrict it to your own account only.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy, or requests regarding your data (access, correction, deletion): appsmovingmeforward@gmail.com
